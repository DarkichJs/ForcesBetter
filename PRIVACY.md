# Privacy Policy

ForcesBetter does not collect or sell any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The extension has no analytics, telemetry, tracking or servers of its own. Nothing is ever sent to the developer or any third party.
- **Running code.** When you press Run or Samples in the code editor, your code and test input are sent to Codeforces' own Custom Invocation (`codeforces.com/data/customtest`) in your logged-in session, exactly as the site's own "Custom test" page does. When you press Submit, your code is handed to the Codeforces submit form, which you send yourself.
- **Settings and drafts.** Your preferences are saved with `chrome.storage.sync`; Chrome may sync them across your signed-in devices. Code drafts, the chosen language and layout sizes are kept in the `localStorage` of codeforces.com on your device.
- **Page access.** The extension runs only on `codeforces.com` and its subdomains. It does not read your messages or account details.
- **Permissions.** `storage` is the only requested permission.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/DarkichJs/ForcesBetter/issues.
