# Privacy Policy

ForcesBetter does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The extension has no analytics, telemetry, tracking or remote servers. It makes no network requests of its own.
- **Settings.** Your preferences (theme, accent color, corner radius, font, page width) are saved with `chrome.storage.sync` and, to avoid a flash of the old theme, in the `localStorage` of codeforces.com. Chrome may sync them across your signed-in devices. They are never sent anywhere else.
- **Page access.** The extension runs only on `codeforces.com` and its subdomains, where it changes the page's appearance. It does not read or send your submissions, messages or account details.
- **Permissions.** `storage` is the only requested permission.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/DarkichJs/ForcesBetter/issues.
